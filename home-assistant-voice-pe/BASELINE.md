# Baseline of the current firmware component

Purpose: numbers of the current component (the one taken over from upstream) to compare with the own rewrite. Same measurements before and after, same conditions.

## How it is measured
- Server side: `/data/metrics.jsonl` in the add on (events `ready`, `first_audio`, `interrupt`, `session_end`), written since add on 2.53.0 (13 Nov 2026). Summary: median and 90th percentile per number.
- Device side: Home Assistant sensors "Leyla free heap", "Leyla largest free block", "Leyla loop time" (a measure for CPU load), "Leyla WiFi signal" and "Leyla uptime" (installed 13 Nov 2026 at 23:35); read their history in Home Assistant. Still to collect: wake to ready on the device, interrupt reaction on the device.
- Wake behaviour: false wakes per day from the event `esphome.wake_word_detected` and the collected false wake recordings.

## Values
First device values (13 Nov 2026, 23:50, 8 minutes after boot, idle): WiFi signal -51 dBm (good), free heap 122 KB, largest free block 111 KB, loop time 17 ms. Look again after a conversation and after a day of uptime: a free heap that keeps sinking means a memory leak, a loop time that jumps during talking shows CPU load.
Microphone level to the server (add on 2.53.x, XMOS AGC on): Leyla speaking about rms 2600 (peak 6900), quiet about rms 380 (peak 860) in the first clean test after the reflash.
With leyla_voice built in but idle (14 Nov 2026, 00:20): free heap 107 KB, largest block 94 KB, loop time 17 ms. Cost of the idle component: about 15 KB heap, no loop time change.
Collection started 13 Nov 2026 at 22:30. Fill in after a few days of normal use.

leyla_voice first conversation (14 Nov 2026, 00:57, 40 s): ready after 1124 ms, first reply audio after 2146 ms, no dropped bytes, free heap during talking 53 KB to 62 KB, after the conversation 81 KB. Server side first_audio after last word 0.01 to 0.1 s. Mic level was hot (rms about 20000) but the old component showed the same in the next conversation, so the level is a device or room matter, not the component. Old component for comparison still to measure with the same wording: ready time on the device and reply time.

Direct comparison on the device (14 Nov 2026, 01:21 to 01:32), old component: WebSocket connected to ready 1.0 to 1.5 s (1.53, 1.07, 1.00). New component: start to ready 1.0 to 1.3 s (1.14, 1.30, 1.03, includes connect), so equal within noise, the time is dominated by the server opening the live session. Free heap after the first conversation is 81 KB with both components (106 KB only before the first conversation after boot), so the 25 KB step is not caused by leyla_voice. Server side after a finished web search it takes about 16 s until the reply starts (old component, 01:26): the biggest speed gain still on the table.

Web search reply time on the server: before add on 2.54.0, 16 s from search completed to reply start (14 Nov 2026 01:26). With backend_reasoning_effort low, 1.6 s (01:37, same question). Answer quality to be watched over the next days.

A/B run with the same question three times in one session (14 Nov 2026, 02:27 to 02:31, server metrics): old component 49.7 s session, ready 1.14 s, reply start 0.01 to 0.1 s after last word, mic speaking rms 3935. New component 35.2 s session, reply start 0.01 to 0.1 s after last word, mic speaking rms 3807. Server side no difference between the components, as expected, because the server decides the reply time. Differences can only show on the device (first reply audio, heap, loop time), see the "Conversation over" log line of the new component.

Device side of the same A/B (14 Nov 2026, device log): old component start to ready 1.16 s, first reply audio 2.19 s after start, free heap while talking 51 KB, after the conversation 81.5 KB, loop time 17 to 20 ms. New component ready 0.98 s, first reply audio 2.02 s, free heap while talking 53 to 62 KB (PSRAM buffers), after the conversation 81.4 KB, loop time 17 to 19 ms, nothing dropped (mic 0, reply 0). Result: new is about 0.2 s faster to ready and 0.2 s faster to first reply, uses up to 11 KB less internal heap during talking, same after. Open: one sample each, repeat for a safe median. Note: the log line "free heap" of the new component shows 6279272 because it counts PSRAM, to be changed to internal heap only.

Second run of the new component after the heap log fix (14 Nov 2026, 02:49): ready 1171 ms, first reply audio 2104 ms, 1902 mic blocks sent, nothing dropped, internal free heap at the end of the conversation 57 KB (log now counts internal heap only). Honest reading of all runs so far: ready time is equal within noise (old 1.0 to 1.5 s, new 0.98 to 1.30 s), first reply about 2.0 to 2.1 s new against 2.19 s old (one old sample, difference too small to claim). Proven advantages of the new component are memory during talking (buffers in PSRAM, 53 to 62 KB against 51 KB), clean error handling (server away: gives up after 20 ms) and the tested pure logic (70 checks); no proven speed gain yet, so do not claim one.

Error test connection lost mid conversation (new component, 14 Nov 2026 02:50, server restarted while talking, stands in for WiFi loss with a closed socket): the device ended the conversation 1.0 s after the restart, buffers released (internal heap back to 81 KB), wake word detection kept running, add on back up (2.54.5 started). Not covered: a silent drop without close frame (real WiFi loss), needs the auto stop timeout or a ping; and the quota message on the device (needs a fake server, the parsing is covered by the unit tests).

Live switch (14 Nov 2026, about 03:08, leyla_voice alone in the config, old component removed): idle internal free heap 145 KB (old component alone before the first conversation 122 KB, so +23 KB), largest free block 131 KB (old 111 KB), loop time 17 ms. First live conversation by wake word: ready 1186 ms, first reply audio 3227 ms (single sample, includes the speaking pause of the user, repeat for a median), nothing dropped, internal heap 120 KB after the conversation against 81 KB with the old component (+39 KB). The idle 15 KB cost seen earlier was the old and new component side by side plus 12 KB of microphone work buffers; the buffers are cut to 1.5 KB in the next build.

After the microphone work buffers were cut from 12 KB to 1.5 KB (14 Nov 2026, about 03:20): idle internal free heap 155 KB (before the cut 145 KB, old component 122 KB, so +33 KB against the old one), largest free block 139 KB (old 111 KB), loop time 17 ms. Still to measure: heap after a conversation and the trend over days.

Conversation by wake word after the buffer cut (14 Nov 2026, 03:23): ready 1267 ms, first reply audio 2288 ms, 999 mic blocks, nothing dropped, internal heap 106 KB at the end and 130 KB a few seconds later (old component after a conversation: 81 KB), largest free block 90 KB after the conversation (old 43 KB).
